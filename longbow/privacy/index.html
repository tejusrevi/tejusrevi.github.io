Longbow Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Longbow is an archery duel game for iPhone. This page explains what the game collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. The short version: playing against the computer sends nothing anywhere, and online play uses only what it needs to match you up and run the duel. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Playing offline

Matches against the computer and pass-and-play matches happen entirely on your phone. Your level, records, hats and archer's look are stored on your phone only.

Playing online

When you open Online, the game creates an account for you on Longbow's game server. That account holds:

WhatWhy
A random ID made by the appTo recognise your phone. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or phone number.
Your gamertag and countryYou choose both. Other players see them in duels.
Your Game Center player ID (only if you are signed in to Game Center)So the same account works on your other devices.
A notification token (only if you allow notifications)To tell you when a friend joins the duel you started.

During an online duel, the server receives each shot (its angle and power) to work out the result. Your archer's look and hat are passed to your opponent so they can see you. None of this is kept after the duel ends.

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your gamertag, country, archer and hat when you duel them.
  • Apple delivers notifications and runs Game Center under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts the game server (in Canada). It stores the data but does not use it.

Nobody else gets your data. It is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

How long it is kept

  • Your account stays until you delete it.
  • Nightly server backups are kept for 14 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within two weeks.
  • Server logs, which can include your account ID, are capped in size and overwritten as they fill, typically within days.

Deleting your account

In the game, open your profile (tap your portrait on the title screen) and tap delete account. This removes your account, gamertag, Game Center link and notification token from the server straight away, and resets your progress on the phone. You can also email the address below to ask for deletion.

Children

Longbow does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the account described above, and that account contains no real-world identity. If you believe a child has created an account and want it removed, email the address below.
